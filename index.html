The bar · เกี่ยวกับเรา

One of the smallest bars on the strip — and one of the most popular.

Vikings sits right on the corner of Soi Sampan, the heart of downtown Udon Thani's nightlife street behind Central Plaza, across from the 7-Eleven. It has been pouring for years and has become a familiar meeting point for expats, visitors and locals alike.

Come by for an afternoon drink, meet up with friends after work, or settle in for the evening. Pull up a stool, order something cold and watch Soi Sampan go by.

  • Open from 8 AM — every day of the week
  • Corner seats with a view of the street
  • Friendly staff, good music, easy company
  • Come on your own — it's easy to make friends here
